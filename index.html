How this works

Proj PRA is the model's expected points + rebounds + assists. In Win, a tie for first counts as a split. Top 3 is the share of nights in which the player finished in the top three, which matters on Three Kings nights. Q marks a questionable / day-to-day player: the model gives them a 70% chance to play, and Proj PRA assumes they play. Out players are left off. The prediction refreshes about every two hours until the first tip-off, then locks; that locked version is the one graded on the Model page.
