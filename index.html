Model win chances

Proj PRA is the model's expected points + rebounds + assists. Win is the share of 10,000 simulated nights in which the player finished first (a tie counts as a split). Top 3 is the share in which they finished in the top three, which matters on Three Kings nights. Q marks a questionable / day-to-day player: the model gives them a 70% chance to play, and Proj PRA assumes they play. Out players are left off. The prediction refreshes about every two hours until the first tip-off, then locks; that locked version is the one graded on the Model page. Click Win, Top 3, Pick % or Value to sort; # stays the model's rank by win chance.
