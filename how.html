ROUND 1 · PROJECTION

Start from recent games

The model starts with each player's games so far this season. Recent games count more: a game's weight halves every 6 games back, so last week counts about twice as much as three weeks ago.

It projects minutes and production per minute separately, and mixes in last season so a few odd games can't swing things too far.

ROUND 3 · NOISE

Every score is a range

A projection is a typical night, not a promise. Franz's usually lands within about points either way. Bigger projections swing more, so Giannis's curve is wider.

Since v3, the curves lean right. A bad night can only get so bad, but a great night has no ceiling. Last season, role players had huge nights about four times as often as an even bell curve predicts.

ROUND 4 · SIMULATE

Play the night 10,000 times

Each simulated night, every player gets a random score from their own curve. The highest score wins that night.

Two details: players in the same game rise or fall together a little (a fast game or overtime helps both teams), and Giannis was listed day-to-day, so the model sits Giannis out 3 simulated nights in 10.

Watch the first few dozen nights: the bars jump around. After a few thousand they barely move. Franz is marked in red.

Why simulate? There's no simple formula for the chance of being the top scorer out of players. So the model plays the night out 10,000 times and counts the wins. That's enough that running it again would move Giannis's by less than half a point.

ROUND 5 · THE CROWD

Guess what the crowd picks

The $1M is split among everyone who picked the winner. So a winning pick pays more when fewer people made it.

DraftKings doesn't show who picked whom before the night. The model estimates it from last season, where each winner's payout showed how many people had picked that player. Fans pick players with a better chance and a higher scoring average, and they pick rookies about 3.4 times as often as a similar veteran.

The mustard mark shows where a player would be if picks matched their chance. Darius Acuff Jr., a rookie, gets an estimated of picks with a chance to win.
